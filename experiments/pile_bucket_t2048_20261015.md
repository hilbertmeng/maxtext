## Runner and validation

`MaxText/pile_bucket_eval.py` prepares integrity-checked mmap arrays from exactly
the original record stream, restores parameters read-only, disables dropout and
reduces token CE to per-sequence/per-bucket sums/counts inside JIT. Float32 device
sums are accumulated in float64 on host. Every batch audits bucket/global sums;
full completion additionally audits independent bucket target counts. Masked dummy
rows and padding may never contaminate valid loss with NaNs.

Progress and compressed per-sequence chunks are uploaded from the worker to GCS.
A replacement worker resumes only a matching dataset/model/runtime/config identity.
Results are downloaded and checked locally before deleting owned TPUs.

CPU test: pinned maxtext-cpu environment, `PYTHONPATH=MaxText`,
`MaxText/tests/pile_bucket_eval_test.py`; three checks passed, covering exact bucket
edges, real EOS, partial tail/batch, dummy NaNs, valid nonfinite CE rejection,
original boundaries and cached-array corruption.

Prior expectation: globalBAM<Mudd<MHA from the matched training endpoint. A larger
BAM advantage at later positions is the directional hypothesis; an advantage
confined to early buckets would contradict that context-benefit explanation.
No training-loss gap is substituted for validation measurements.
